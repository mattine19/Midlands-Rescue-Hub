Software Design & UML Architecture

Connecting Animals in Need With People Who Care.

Midlands Rescue Hub is a proposed platform designed to help animal rescues, shelters, and veterinary clinics coordinate assistance and connect animals with suitable fosters and volunteers.

Explore Our System Design

About the Project

Animal rescue organizations often need to coordinate foster placements, volunteers, and other resources quickly. Midlands Rescue Hub is designed to organize animal information, manage placement requests, identify potential volunteer matches, and coordinate assistance through a structured software system.

How the System Works

The UML design organizes the system into components that each handle a specific responsibility.

1

Animal

Stores information about an animal and its placement or care needs.

2

Placement Request

Represents a request for assistance, such as finding a foster or volunteer.

3

Matching Engine

Identifies potential helpers based on availability, experience, and preferences.

4

Dispatch Service

Coordinates potential matches and the next steps in the assistance process.

5

Organization

Represents the rescues, shelters, and clinics involved in coordinating animal care.

UML System Components

Each component contributes to the proposed architecture of Midlands Rescue Hub.

Data Model

Animal

Represents animals and the information needed to understand their care and placement requirements.

Data Model

Volunteer

Represents potential helpers, including their experience, preferences, and availability.

Matching

MatchingEngine

Evaluates potential matches between assistance requests and suitable volunteers.

Workflow

PlacementRequest

Represents a request for assistance with an animal that needs support.

Workflow

DispatchService

Represents the coordination process for potential matches and follow-up actions.

Organizations

Organization

Represents participating animal rescues, shelters, veterinary clinics, and other groups.

Data Handling

DataLoader / DataWriter

Represent the planned responsibilities for loading and saving system data.

Collections

RequestList / VolunteerList

Organize placement requests and volunteer records for use by the system.

Application Layer

RescueHubFacade

Provides a central interface for coordinating the system's main operations.

Project Status: This website presents the project concept and intended UML architecture. It is a design showcase, not a claim that all components are connected to a live database or operational animal rescue service.
