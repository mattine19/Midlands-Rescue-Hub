Helping Rescues Work Together

Midlands Rescue Hub connects animal rescues, shelters, and veterinary clinics with available fosters and volunteers. Our goal is to make it easier to coordinate urgent animal care and help more animals find the support they need.

How It Works

🐾 Submit a Request

Organizations identify animals that need urgent assistance.

🤝 Match With Helpers

Find suitable fosters and volunteers based on availability and experience.

❤️ Coordinate Care

Help organizations connect animals with the resources they need.

Our Mission

We aim to support animal welfare organizations across the Midlands and eventually throughout South Carolina.
